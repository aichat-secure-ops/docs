## **Support Ticket Guide**

The Support Ticket helps your team handle customer requests efficiently, from assignment to resolution. Whether you’re responding to tickets or configuring workflows, everything is in one place.

**Overview**

Support Ticket helps your team manage customer requests from creation to resolution. Tickets are created from chatbot conversations when users request help and provide their details. Tickets can be accessed and managed from the **Tickets module** in the CMS.

## For Agents: Handling Requests

Quickly respond, track and resolve customer inquiries.

### **Viewing Tickets**

- Agents can view tickets in the **Tickets dashboard**, where tickets are organized by status: All, In Progress, Pending, Snoozed, Resolved
- Each ticket shows key details such as: Ticket ID, Reason ,Topic, Status, Assignee, SLA

### **Tasks You Can Do**

**Receive & Manage Requests**

- Tickets are created when customers request help and provide their details (name, email, phone, reason).
- Tickets may be assigned automatically or manually.
- Get in-app notifications and email alerts for new tickets—during or after working hours.

**Work On Requests**

- View full conversation history and customer details.
- Use AI-suggested replies, shortcuts, and attachments to respond faster.
- Manage multiple tickets based on your capacity.

**Pause Requests**

- Snooze tickets to pause work (e.g., while investigating).
- Snoozed tickets don’t count toward your active ticket limit.

**Reassign or Unassign Requests**

- Reassign tickets to another agent or team if needed.
- Unassign tickets if unavailable or not the right person—supervisors will reassign automatically.
- Unassigned tickets return to the queue for others.

**Resolve Requests**

- Click **“Resolve Ticket”** to close completed cases.
- Pick another ticket from the queue once resolved.
- Resolution contributes to SLA targets and performance metrics.

**SLA**

- SLA status is shown on each ticket and helps prioritize response and resolution.

## For Admins & Supervisors: Configuring Workflows

Set up teams, routes, and policies to keep support running smoothly.

### **Settings You Can Customize**

**Create & Hand Off Requests**

- Turn chatbot conversations into tickets automatically.
- Customize: trigger phrases, required info, confirmation/error messages, and agent notifications.

![Ticketing Flow (1) (1) 1](/images/ticketing_flow-\(1\)-\(1\)-1.gif "Ticketing Flow (1) (1) 1")

- Full conversation history is logged for seamless tracking and reporting.
- Future feature: topic-based ticket assignment.

**Manage Support Teams**

- Add agents and supervisors, assign skills, and track performance.
- Teams inherit global settings but can override them if needed.
- Check agent availability:
  - 🟢 Online
  - 🔴 Offline

**Assignment Rules & Capacity**

![Image](/images/image-1.png "Image")

- Choose how tickets are assigned: manual or load-balanced.
- Set max tickets per agent.
- Control queue behavior when agents reach capacity.

**Operating Hours & Holidays**

![Operation (2) 1](/images/Operation-\(2\)-1.gif "Operation (2) 1")

- Define working hours globally or per team.
- Set time zones for SLA accuracy.
- Configure holidays—tickets created outside hours remain queued.

**SLA Policies**

![SLA (2) (1) 1](/images/SLA-\(2\)-\(1\)-1.gif "SLA (2) (1) 1")

- Set targets: First Response Time (FRT), Next Response Time (NRT), Resolution Time (RT).
- Track compliance, breaches, and escalations.
- SLA is tracked at the ticket level and used to monitor response and resolution performance.

**Notifications**

- Alerts for: assignment/reassignment, after-hours requests, unassigned tickets, SLA breaches, and queue backlogs.

**Queue & Customer Experience**

![Q (1) 1](/images/Q-\(1\)-1.gif "Q (1) 1")

- Customize queue messages: estimated wait time, position, max wait time.
- Let customers exit the queue with confirmation messages.

**Productivity & Governance**

- Manage shortcuts for consistent responses.
- Review ticket topics for routing and reporting.
- Track analytics on: ticket volume, queue performance, SLA compliance, agent performance, and CSAT.
- Analytics can be viewed in the Analytics dashboard for monitoring performance and trends.

## **Tips for Agents and Admins**

- **Agents:** Use AI-suggested replies and shortcuts to speed up responses.
- **Admins:** Regularly review SLA metrics and queue performance to keep response times fast.
- **Both:** Snooze tickets instead of leaving them incomplete—it keeps the queue organized.
