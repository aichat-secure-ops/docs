---
title: "Enable Ticketing"
---

Before using Ticketing, create a support team and add the agents who will handle customer requests.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Support Team">
     Go to **Settings → Support Teams →+Create Team → Create Team.** Add suitable name and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Agents in the Support Team">
    Click the Support Team created, go to **Add Agents** and select **required agents** to be in the team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
